Madurai Property Due-Diligence Checklist

Use this checklist during document review and your site visit. Have an independent legal or technical professional verify the details.

Property and seller

  • Seller or developer identity confirmed
  • Authority to sell confirmed
  • Parent or chain-of-title documents reviewed
  • Survey number, boundaries and extent match the property

Approvals and legal checks

  • Encumbrance Certificate reviewed for the relevant period
  • Patta and land classification checked where applicable
  • Layout or building approval number recorded
  • RERA registration checked where applicable
  • Property tax and statutory dues checked

Site and infrastructure

  • Approach road and access rights inspected
  • Water, drainage and electricity confirmed
  • Boundaries and common areas inspected
  • Completed infrastructure separated from proposed infrastructure
  • Photos and notes recorded with permission

Money and agreement

  • Complete cost sheet received
  • Registration, development, maintenance and other charges understood
  • Payment schedule and cancellation terms reviewed
  • Possession or handover terms recorded
  • Independent legal review completed before token payment
